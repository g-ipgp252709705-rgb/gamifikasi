### MICRO QUEST: THE GREAT YEAST INVESTIGATION 🔬

**Micro Quest: The Great Yeast Investigation** is an interactive digital learning experience designed to make learning about microorganisms more engaging, visual and enjoyable. Through a science-investigation theme, learners explore the fascinating world of yeast and develop a better understanding of its characteristics and activities.

The application combines interactive elements, animated visual effects, sound and gesture-recognition technology to create an immersive learning experience. Learners can investigate scientific concepts through digital interaction rather than relying solely on traditional text-based explanations.

Designed to support science education, Micro Quest encourages curiosity, active participation, observation and scientific thinking. It also demonstrates how digital technology can be integrated into teaching and learning to transform abstract scientific concepts into a more engaging experience.

**Learning focus:** Yeast, microorganisms, scientific investigation and technology-enhanced learning.

**Target audience:** Primary school learners and anyone interested in exploring microorganisms through interactive digital learning.

**How to use:** Open the application, follow the on-screen instructions and interact with the investigation activities. If camera-based gesture recognition is required, allow camera access when prompted.
